Welcome to the gig.

The Minstrel is a sword-and-sorcery comedy told in the spirit of the newspaper comic strips of the 1980s: colourful characters, quick jokes, ongoing adventures, and just enough danger to make bringing a lute into a tomb seem like a bad idea.
